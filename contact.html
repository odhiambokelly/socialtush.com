Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our consultation process and services.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, completely free with no strings attached. We'll discuss your goals, analyze your current situation, and provide actionable recommendations. You can decide afterward if you want to work with us.

How long does the consultation take?

Typically 30-45 minutes. We'll review your current social media presence, discuss your goals, and present a customized strategy for your business.

What do I need to prepare?

Just your goals and any questions you have. If you have existing social media accounts, we'll do a quick audit during the call. No preparation required on your end.

Do you work with businesses in my industry?

We've worked with businesses across 20+ industries including e-commerce, SaaS, healthcare, finance, education, and more. Our strategies are customized for each client.

When can we start working together?

We can typically start within 1-2 weeks of your consultation, depending on your timeline and the scope of work. Rush projects can sometimes be accommodated.
