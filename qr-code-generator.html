QR code types explained

  
URL to QR code

  
Turn any web address into a QR code that opens the page when scanned. Great for business cards, product packaging and posters.

  
Wi-Fi QR code generator

  
Guests scan the code to join your Wi-Fi without typing the password. Most iPhone and Android cameras support it. Choose WPA/WPA2 for modern routers, and tick "Hidden network" only if your network name is not broadcast.

  
WhatsApp QR code generator

  
Creates a https://wa.me/ link that opens a chat with your number, optionally with a ready-written message. Perfect for shops, service businesses and customer support.

  
Contact card (vCard) QR code

  
Stores a name, phone, email, company, website and address. Scanning it offers to save the contact to the phone, which makes it handy on business cards and at events.

  
Email, phone, SMS and location

  
Open a pre-filled email, start a phone call, prepare a text message, or show a place on a map with a single scan.

Tips for QR codes that always scan

  
    
    
  • Use a dark code on a light background with strong contrast.
    • 
    
  • Keep the margin (quiet zone) of about 4 modules around the code.
    • 
    
  • Print it at least 2 × 2 cm, and larger for posters seen from a distance.
    • 
    
  • Choose error correction Q or H if the code may get scratched or dirty.
    • 
  


  
Privacy

  
Your QR codes are generated on your device. What you type, including Wi-Fi passwords and contact details, is never sent to SocialTush or anyone else, never stored and never logged.


  
Frequently asked questions

  
Is this QR code generator really free?
Yes. You can create unlimited QR codes and download them as PNG or SVG with no sign-up, no watermark and no download limits.


Do the QR codes expire?
No. These are static QR codes: the information is stored inside the code itself, so they keep working for as long as the content (for example your website) exists.


How do I create a Wi-Fi QR code?
Choose Wi-Fi, enter the network name, password and security type, then download the code. Guests can scan it with their phone camera to join the network without typing the password.


How do I make a WhatsApp QR code?
Choose WhatsApp, enter the number with its country code (for example 254712345678) and an optional message. Scanning the code opens a WhatsApp chat with that number.


Should I download PNG or SVG?
PNG works everywhere, including documents and social media. SVG is a vector file that stays sharp at any size, which is best for printing on flyers, banners and packaging.


Is my information private?
Yes. QR codes are generated in your browser. What you type, including Wi-Fi passwords and contact details, is never sent to SocialTush or stored.
