How this email checker works

Every check runs real lookups. Nothing is guessed or simulated:

  • Format: the address is checked against email syntax rules, and common typos such as gmial.com are spotted.
  • Domain and DNS: we query public DNS to confirm the domain exists and read its MX (mail server), SPF and DMARC records.
  • Disposable detection: the domain, and the mail servers it uses, are compared with our database of more than 10,000 disposable email domains (updated weekly from an open-source list) and with live services from Kickbox, DeBounce and mailcheck.ai.
  • Classification: we flag role addresses (admin@, info@, sales@), free providers (Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook) and domains linked to spam.

What is a disposable email address?

Disposable or temporary email services hand out inboxes that self-destruct after minutes or days. They are popular for avoiding sign-up confirmations, but messages sent to them later are lost, and they are often used for fake accounts or abuse. A disposable email checker helps you keep sign-up lists, leads and customer records clean.

Email deliverability: deliverable, undeliverable or risky

Undeliverable

The address cannot receive email: the format is invalid, the domain does not exist, or the domain has no mail servers (or publishes a "null MX" that refuses mail).

Risky

Mail might not arrive or might not reach a real person: disposable and spam-linked domains, catch-all domains that accept every address, and mailboxes that could not be individually verified.

Deliverable

Only shown when the recipient's mail server confirms the mailbox in an SMTP-level check. A valid format and working MX records are necessary, but they do not prove that a specific mailbox exists.

MX, SPF and DMARC records explained

MX records list the servers that receive email for a domain; without them, mail has nowhere to go. SPF lists the servers allowed to send email for the domain, and DMARC tells receivers what to do with mail that fails those checks. Well-run business domains usually publish all three.

Role-based and catch-all addresses

Role addresses such as info@ or support@ reach a team or shared inbox rather than one person, which matters for sales outreach and sign-ups. A catch-all domain accepts mail for any address, so a mail server cannot confirm that a specific mailbox exists.

Your privacy

We never send an email to the address you check and we do not store it. For disposable checks, only the domain (for example example.com) is shared with the disposable-email services. Results may be held in temporary memory for a few minutes to avoid repeat lookups.

Frequently asked questions

How do I check if an email address is disposable?

Enter the address in the SocialTush email checker and press Check Email. The domain is checked against our database of over 10,000 disposable email domains and several live disposable-email services, and the result is shown as Disposable email or Not disposable.

How can I check if an email address is real?

The checker confirms that the format is valid, that the domain exists and that it has working mail (MX) servers. Confirming that the individual mailbox exists requires an SMTP-level check by an email verification service; until that check is run, the result says the mailbox is not verified.

What is a disposable or temporary email address?

A disposable email address comes from a service such as a 10-minute mail or burner inbox. It works for a short time and then expires, so messages sent to it later are lost. Businesses often block them at sign-up.

Does a valid MX record mean the email address exists?

No. MX records only show that the domain can receive email. A mailbox can still be missing, full or disabled, which is why the checker does not mark an address as deliverable from MX records alone.

What does a risky email result mean?

Risky means delivery is not certain: for example a disposable or spam-related domain, a catch-all domain that accepts every address, or a mailbox that could not be verified.

Do you send an email to the address I check?

No. The checker never sends any message to the address and does not store the addresses you check.
